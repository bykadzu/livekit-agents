**Target:** 40 paying real estate agents across UK, Spain, France, Germany

### Goals
- 🎯 **5 beta agents** signed up (1-2 per market)
- 🎯 Technical infrastructure deployed and stable
- 🎯 First A-tier lead qualified and viewing booked

- [ ] Set up Langfuse/PostHog for conversation analytics
- [ ] Create agent dashboard (real-time call metrics)
- [ ] Configure alerting (failed calls, low lead scores, etc.)

### Day 4-7: Beta Agent Recruitment

**Target:** 5 beta agents (hand-picked, high-potential)

**Outreach Strategy:**
1. **LinkedIn Direct Messages** (50/day)
   - Target: Agents in London, Barcelona, Paris, Munich with 500+ connections
   - Message template:
     ```
     Hi [Name], I noticed you're active in [London Zone 1]. Quick question:
     How many hours/week do you spend screening leads who never book viewings?

     We've built an AI assistant that handles that for you - would love to
     show you a 2-min demo. Interested?
     ```

2. **Real Estate Facebook Groups** (10 posts/day)
   - Join: "UK Estate Agents Network", "Agentes Inmobiliarios España", etc.
   - Post: "Beta testing an AI that qualifies leads 24/7. Free for first 5 agents. DM me."

3. **Cold Email** (100/day)
   - Scrape emails from agent websites (Rightmove, Zoopla listings)
   - Subject: "Save 10 hours/week screening tire-kickers (AI demo inside)"

**Beta Agent Criteria:**
- Closes 1-2+ deals/month (proven track record)
- Active on LinkedIn/Instagram (will share results organically)
- Works in Tier 1 market (London, Barcelona, Paris, Munich)
- Tech-savvy (can handle 15-min setup)

**Beta Incentives:**
- ✅ Free for 60 days (no commission charges)
- ✅ Lifetime 50% discount if they provide video testimonial
- ✅ €500 referral bonus for every agent they bring in

### Day 8-14: Onboard Beta Agents & Iterate

**Onboarding Process (Per Agent):**
1. **Kickoff Call** (30 min)
   - Explain how it works
   - Set expectations: "You'll start seeing leads within 48 hours"
   - Collect: Phone number, calendar link, MLS area, sample listings

2. **Technical Setup** (15 min)
   - Forward business line to LiveKit number
   - Connect Google Calendar/Calendly
   - Upload 5 sample listings to agent dashboard

3. **Test Call** (5 min)
   - Agent calls their own number
   - Experiences AI firsthand
   - Provides feedback on voice, tone, questions

4. **Go Live**
   - AI starts answering calls within 24 hours
   - Success Manager monitors first 10 calls
   - Daily check-ins for first week

**Iteration:**
- Collect feedback after every 20 calls
- Adjust tone, qualification questions, booking flow
- Fix bugs in real-time (voice latency, misheard words, etc.)

**Success Criteria (End of Week 2):**
- ✅ 5 agents live and taking calls
- ✅ At least 1 A-tier lead qualified
- ✅ At least 1 viewing booked
- ✅ Zero critical bugs (call drops, GDPR violations, etc.)

### Day 43-49: Product Optimization (Based on Data)

**Analyze First 1,000 Calls:**
- What % of leads are qualified as A/B/C?
- What questions lead to best conversion?
- Where do leads drop off? (e.g., asking for email too early)
- What time of day has highest call volume?

**Optimize:**
1. **Qualification Flow:**
   - If <30% A/B leads → Loosen criteria
   - If >70% A leads → Tighten criteria (filtering too many)

2. **Voice & Tone:**
   - If agents report "too robotic" → Adjust TTS speed, add filler words ("um", "you know")
   - If agents report "too casual" → Make more professional

3. **Booking Conversion:**
   - If <40% of A-tier leads book viewing → Simplify booking flow
   - Add incentive: "I can book you for tomorrow at 2pm - does that work?"

4. **GDPR Compliance:**
   - Audit 100 random calls for consent collection
   - Ensure 100% compliance (critical for EU)

### Day 50-56: MLS Integration (Real Data)

**Integrations:**

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Low Agent Adoption
**Mitigation:**
- Focus on beta testimonials (social proof)
- Offer 30-day free trial (zero risk)
- Partner with brokerages (built-in distribution)

### Risk 2: GDPR Violations
**Mitigation:**
- Legal review of all consent flows
- Audit 100% of calls for compliance
- Store data in EU only (no US servers)

### Risk 3: Poor Lead Quality (Too Many C-Tier Leads)
**Mitigation:**
- Iterate on qualification criteria weekly
- Allow agents to customize thresholds
- Provide "why scored C" reasoning (transparency)

### Risk 4: Voice Quality Issues (Latency, Robotic)
**Mitigation:**
- Use premium TTS (ElevenLabs, not Cartesia)
- Optimize for <300ms latency
- Test in all markets (network conditions vary)

### Risk 5: Churn (Agents Cancel After Trial)
**Mitigation:**
- High-touch onboarding (daily check-ins first week)
- Show value fast (first qualified lead within 48 hours)
- Proactive support (CSM reaches out if no activity)
